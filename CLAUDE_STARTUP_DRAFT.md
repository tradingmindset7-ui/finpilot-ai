# Claude startup application preparation — draft

**Project:** FinPilot AI (working name; not yet trademark-checked)

**Status:** Early prototype. Do not describe it as launched, revenue-generating, or having users unless those facts become true.

## Product summary

FinPilot AI is an educational finance assistant designed to help learners understand financial statements, valuation metrics, and investing concepts through clear, accessible explanations. The planned product will emphasise transparent calculations, source-grounded document explanations, and clear disclosure of uncertainty and limitations.

## Planned use of Claude

We plan to evaluate Claude for summarising financial documents, explaining financial metrics, and answering questions grounded in user-provided documents. We will test for factual accuracy, unsupported claims, and consistency, and will clearly distinguish educational content from personalised financial advice.

## Before submitting

- Check whether the official Claude for Startups programme is accepting applications and confirm the current benefits and eligibility criteria.
- Use accurate legal entity, ownership, product-stage, and contact information.
- Add a real working demo URL after deployment.
- Explain why Claude is needed, what is built so far, and what API usage the credits would support.
- Never claim users, revenue, partnerships, compliance, or capabilities that do not exist.
- Do not pay a third party promising guaranteed credits.
